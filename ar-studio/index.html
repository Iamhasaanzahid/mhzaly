Questions

Good to know.

Do people need to install an app?

No. KITE experiences open in the phone's browser — Safari on iOS, Chrome on Android. The “View in AR” button hands off to the device's native AR viewer where supported.

Which 3D formats can I upload?

GLB is the safest choice and the format the studio is built around. GLTF works when the file is self-contained. You can also skip 3D files entirely and start from a prompt, a photo, a word or an SVG.

Does it work on a desktop?

Yes. The studio, the 3D preview and the QR generator all work on desktop. The AR placement step needs a phone camera, so desktop shows a QR to hand the experience to your phone.

Can I use my own logo or artwork?

Yes. Use the Picture or Image format for flat artwork, or upload an SVG in the SVG to 3D format to extrude it into a solid object. Images stay in your browser in this demo.

Is the QR code permanent?

In this demo the QR points to a local view page. In production, each published experience gets a stable link and QR that you can print, embed or share. Unlimited scans are included on every plan.

What about privacy?

This demo does all of its fitting, rendering and QR generation in your browser. Uploaded files never leave the device unless you publish them to your own storage.
