1. Inventory: phone, accessory aur parts alag taur par track karein

Mobile shop mein stock teen tarah ka hota hai: phones, accessories aur repair parts. Har category ka hisaab alag hota hai. Phone ka IMEI, purchase price, warranty aur supplier — yeh sab record hona chahiye. Accessories mein quantity aur fast-moving items matter karte hain. Repair parts mein chhoti quantity hoti hai lekin cost zyada hoti hai.

Ek achha system har product ka stock, low-stock alert aur purchase history dikhata hai. Jab stock khatam hone lagta hai to pehle se pata chal jata hai — customer ko "kal aana" nahi kehna padta.

2. Repair jobs: har phone ka status saaf rakhein

Repair ka kaam sabse zyada confusion paida karta hai. Customer phone chhor kar jata hai, parts mangwane padte hain, aur status poochne ke liye bar bar call aati hai. Agar job card nahi hai to phone, fault aur customer ka record milana mushkil ho jata hai.

Repair job mein yeh cheezein zaroor likhein:

  • Customer ka naam, phone aur device model
  • Fault aur customer ki permission (data backup, parts change)
  • Parts ka cost aur labour charge
  • Status: received, diagnosing, waiting for part, ready, delivered
  • Warranty period (agar diya hai)

Jab status system mein hota hai to customer ko update dena asaan hota hai — aur dukaan ka professional image banta hai.

3. Customer khata: udhaar aur wasooli ka record

Pakistan ki dukano mein udhaar common hai. Lekin agar khata sirf register mein hai to purani entry dhoondna mushkil hota hai, aur kuch customers bhool bhi jate hain. Digital khata mein har customer ka balance, purani sales aur wasooli ka record hota hai.

Zaroori baat: udhaar dene se pehle customer ka phone number aur naam save karein. Isse reminder aur record dono asaan hote hain.

4. Suppliers aur purchases

Supplier ledger se pata chalta hai ke kis supplier ko kitna dena hai aur kis se kitna lena hai. Purchase order, delivery aur payment — teeno alag hote hain. Agar yeh record nahi hai to mahine ke end par hisaab milana mushkil hota hai.

5. Roz ka profit, sirf sale nahi

Sale zyada hona kaafi nahi. Asli sawal yeh hai: profit kitna hua? Iske liye sale, cost of goods, expenses aur returns — sab ek jagah chahiye. Roz ka closing aur mahine ki report se pata chalta hai ke kaunsi category profit de rahi hai aur kahan paisa leak ho raha hai.
