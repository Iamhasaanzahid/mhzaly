Customer khata digital — udhaar aur wasooli ka simple system

Published 18 November 2026 • 5 min read • by mhzaly

Pakistan ki dukano mein udhaar aam baat hai. Lekin jab khata sirf register mein ho, to purani entry dhoondna, balance batana aur wasooli karna mushkil ho jata hai. Digital khata in teeno kaam ko asaan banata hai.

Customer khata kya hota hai?

Customer khata har customer ka record hai: kya kharida, kitne paise diye, kitna baqi hai aur kab wasooli hui. Yeh sale record se alag cheez hai — sale har customer ki hoti hai, lekin udhaar sirf kuch customers ka hota hai.

Digital khata kaise banayein

  1. Har customer ka naam aur phone number save karein. Bina number ke baad mein identify karna mushkil hota hai.
  2. Udhaar dene se pehle balance check karein. Purana balance clear hone se pehle naya udhaar na dein — yeh rule likh kar rakhein.
  3. Har payment ka record banayein. Cash, JazzCash ya bank — jo bhi ho, entry karein.
  4. Hafte mein ek baar balance list dekhein. Purane udhaar par polite reminder bhejein.
  5. Customer ko receipt ya message dein. Isse confusion kam hota hai aur trust banta hai.
